# What Brehon Consensus actually does — plain-English extract

Source: `docs/brehon-law-inspired-network/` (01-vision, 02-domain-model,
expert-review-suite/01-system-overview) + founder's own framing 2026-06-27.
This is the *ground truth* the messaging must stay faithful to. Technically
correct, plainly stated.

## Scope — what it does and ONLY what it does

The founder's own words: **"it only handles membership and moderation in a
group activity."** That narrowness is a strength, not a limitation. It does two
things, well:

1. **Membership** — who gets to belong, and how trust is built.
2. **Moderation** — what happens when someone breaks the group's rules, decided
   fairly and openly.

It is **not** a social network, a chat app, or a content platform. It's the
**rules-and-fairness layer** that sits on top of those. The group makes the
content; Brehon Consensus governs how the group governs itself.

## The product in plain language (no jargon)

| What the docs call it | What it actually means to a person |
|---|---|
| Sponsorship / surety | You get in because real members vouch for you — like a reference for a job or a club. Your sponsors stake a bit of their own standing on you. |
| Reputation (4 dimensions, event-sourced) | Standing you earn slowly through your actions. Not a single score or leaderboard — it just unlocks what you're trusted to do. **Earned by *helping* (today: jury service — volunteering to weigh cases by the community's rules), NOT by posting/liking/sharing. Engagement ≠ standing.** See themes/10. |
| Capabilities (not scores) | The system shows "trusted reporter" or "can sit on a jury", not a number. Abilities, earned. |
| Jury (5 members, drawn from the pool) | When something serious happens, ordinary members — not bosses — are picked to decide it. Like jury duty. |
| Graduated / restorative sanctions | Consequences escalate gently: a note, then reduced reach, then a pause — repair before exclusion. Bans are the last resort, not the reflex. This is not Cancel Culture! |
| Public case log (redacted) | Every serious decision is written down in the open: which rule, what was decided, why. No secret moderation. |
| Appeals | One guaranteed appeal, heard by a new, bigger jury. Get it wrong and the original jurors lose standing. |
| Federation (advisory signals) | Communities can warn each other, but no community can be forced to obey another. Local rule stays local. |
| Customisable rules | **The group writes its own rules.** The app is fully customisable — every community decides what belongs and what doesn't, for itself. |

## The five qualities the founder names

From the founder, verbatim: the system is **transparent, open, democratic,
consensus-based, and inclusive**, and following the Brehon tradition it seeks
**restorative justice** — it **"avoids punitive and needless punishment and pain
and suffering."**

These six words are the emotional core and should survive into every version of
the copy:

- **Transparent** — decisions are visible, with reasons.
- **Open** — open source, open process, anyone can inspect it.
- **Democratic** — power comes from members, not admins.
- **Consensus-based** — agreement, juries, thresholds — not one person's say-so.
- **Inclusive** — reintegration over banishment; no permanent outcasts.
- **Restorative** — repair the harm, don't just punish the person.

## The three "dials" everything turns on (from 01-vision §8)

Useful internally, maybe too abstract for the pamphlet, but it's the real
engine:

1. Make identity **costly to abandon** (you'd lose your earned standing).
2. Make trust **slow to build** (no instant power).
3. Make reputation **worth protecting** (it unlocks things; your sponsors share
   the risk).

## What it explicitly is NOT (guard-rails for honest copy)

- **Not** crypto / tokens / "more money = more power." Influence is earned, never
  bought.
- **Not** a blockchain-of-everything. (A narrow public-memory anchor is a
  *maybe-someday* V3, evidential only — do **not** lead with blockchain; it
  misleads.)
- **Not** permanent bans by default.
- **Not** a single reputation score / leaderboard.
- **Not** rule-by-the-loudest or rule-by-the-mob — procedure protects against
  brigading and cliques alike.
