## The ten messaging rules (evidence-backed)

1. **Lead with the power contrast, not the mechanism.** Who holds power, and how
   it changes — "your rules, not the platform's" — before any category noun.
2. **Name the pain before the solution.** Organisers live the problem (bots,
   pile-ons, burnout); meet exhaustion with relief, never a lecture.
3. **Explain the unfamiliar through one concrete everyday analogy** — and, for the
   core idea, *two* from different domains (email + jury). (theme 04)
4. **Concrete verbs, plain words, short sentences.** Cut the jargon; it reads as
   "not for you," not as intelligence. (theme 07)
5. **Avoid "governance" as the headline noun** — it triggers institutional
   derision. Lead with "run your group" + "community jury". (themes 02, 08)
6. **Frame standing as earned by *helping* govern, never a score.** (theme 10)
7. **Earn trust by showing your working and admitting limits** — for a solo-dev
   alpha, honesty is a *credibility asset* (the pratfall effect). (theme 07)
8. **Pre-empt the crypto misread before the reader raises it** — no blockchain, no
   token, no coin; "decentralised" = works like email. (themes 07, 10)
9. **Define accountability as active repair; evoke the cancel-culture experience
   without the label.** Never print the phrase "cancel culture". (theme 09)
10. **Make fair process the promise; end on agency and hope.** (themes 05, 13)

## Positioning angles (research-ranked)

The research's recommended lead is "accountability without annihilation" — but
that label is **internal only**; in our voice the angles are:

- **Angle 1 (research's top pick — warmest, most-evidenced):** fair process for
  self-governing communities — *your members handle it by your rules, repair not
  exile, a way back.* Rests on Tyler's 30+ years of procedural-justice evidence
  (people accept outcomes they dislike when the process was fair). (theme 09)
- **Angle 2 (strong second beat):** *rules you wrote, kept by people you trust* —
  consent and self-determination (Ostrom). Resonates hardest with co-ops/unions.
  (themes 10, 14)
- **Angle 3 (use with care):** *an old idea for a new problem* — highest risk of
  Celtic-heritage romance; lead with mechanism, keep Brehon as a footnote. (theme 03)

> ⚠ Which angle *leads* the pamphlet is still an open founder call (deferred — see
> the lead-angle fork). The evidence favours Angle 1; the bottom-up spine (theme
> 14) frames all three.

## The five risk mitigations (what to avoid, and how)

- **Reputation-as-surveillance / social-credit misread** → anchor standing in
  contribution; state the negatives ("not a popularity score, not earned by
  posting"); name the *Black Mirror: Nosedive* fear and dismiss it. (theme 10)
- **Cancel-culture / culture-war trap** → never use the term; evoke the
  experience (unilateral exile, no due process, no way back); pre-empt "soft on
  crime" with active-accountability framing (repair is *harder* than a ban).
  (theme 09)
- **Crypto / blockchain misassociation** → pre-emptive disassociation up front,
  not a buried FAQ; lead the "decentralised" explanation with the email analogy.
  (theme 07)
- **Over-claiming at the alpha stage** → state plainly it's early/experimental;
  name what it doesn't do yet; replace superlatives with specifics; show your
  working (open code, public reasoning). (theme 07)
- **Celtic-heritage romance** → Brehon as one example among many, cited soberly
  with its bad parts (slavery, hierarchy); lead mechanism, not origin story.
  (theme 03)

## What the research settled (load-bearing facts)

- **History (Section E):** Brehon ran centuries with no state/police/prisons
  (SOLID — Kelly, Stacey, Biggins); Brehon was bottom-up (SOLID — Biggins);
  Graeber/Wengrow solid in general, contested on specifics. **No Brehon-specific
  modern court case exists** — citable customary-law cases (Mabo, Ellis,
  Tsilhqot'in) are all First Nations/Indigenous, none Irish. (theme 03)
- **Live-vs-design (repo check):** jury draw (random + diversity-aware), juror
  cooldown, reporter-reward, vouching/endorsement, time-based entry, and the
  per-community config knobs are all **LIVE in v0**. The one exception:
  reputation **decay** is built but **off by default** — always "designed to /
  can be enabled", never "fades". (themes 10, 11, 12, 13, 15)

## Contested claims + message-testing recommendations

- **Test the category noun on real organisers before print** — "community jury" /
  "run your group fairly". The first label a reader meets dominates comprehension
  permanently (Moreau 2001), so this single word is worth A/B testing. (theme 02)
- **Cold-test the lead analogies** on 3–5 people from the audience who haven't
  read the themes — the curse of knowledge is invisible; internal review can't
  substitute. (theme 04)
- **Don't assert specific Graeber/Wengrow archaeological facts** as settled
  (critics: Morris, Feinman, Bell) — hedge to the general argument. (theme 03)
