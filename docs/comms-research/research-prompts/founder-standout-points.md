## SP-1 — Make the invisible visible: self-governance "common sense" is ideology, not natural law

**Founder, 2026-06-27 (verbatim framing):**

> Brehon Consensus can be positioned as **making visible what the "common sense"
> of platform governance has rendered invisible**: that communities are capable of
> self-governing, that this has been done throughout history, and that the
> assumption that you need a platform corporation or a technical expert to manage
> group decisions is **an ideological imposition, not a natural law.**

**Why it's strong:** sharper and more *political* than the current anti-TINA frame.
TINA says "no alternative exists"; this goes further — it names the belief itself
as **manufactured common sense** (an ideology), and the product's job as
*denaturalising* it. "You need a corporation / an expert to run your group" feels
like gravity; it's actually a choice that was sold to us.

**Where it likely lives:** deepens [`themes/03-anti-tina.md`](../themes/03-anti-tina.md)
(the spine) and possibly the [`themes/14-bottom-up.md`](../themes/14-bottom-up.md)
"power flows up, not down" layer. Connects to research Section B (Lakoff/framing —
reframing "common sense" is exactly the documented mechanism) and Section C2
(Ostrom — communities demonstrably CAN self-govern the commons; the "tragedy"
framing was the ideological default she overturned).

**Tone caution:** the word "ideological" is powerful but can read as preachy /
academic to a non-technical organiser. Likely usage: keep the *idea* (it's a
choice, not a law of nature; you're more capable than you've been told) but
deliver it in plain, empowering language. The crisp public line is probably closer
to: **"You don't need a tech company to run your community fairly. You never
did."** Founder to confirm how hard/explicit to make the ideology framing.

**Status:** flagged excellent by founder; to incorporate on founder's direction.
The Ostrom + Lakoff support is verifiable in the research (Sections C2 + B) — good,
because it means this frame rests on cited evidence, not just assertion.

## Working method (binding for the whole reconcile) — interpret to OUR layer

**Founder, 2026-06-27:** the research tells us *what works rhetorically*; it does
not give us copy. Two rules govern how every finding gets incorporated:

1. **Re-express to the Brehon Consensus layer, in our own voice.** A research
   label (e.g. "accountability without annihilation") is **internal strategy only,
   never public copy.** Public lines are rebuilt around *what our layer actually
   brings* — member-drawn jury · judged by the community's own rules · repair-first
   with a way back · earned standing · invitation/surety. The mechanic is the
   message; the relief is the consequence.
2. **Themes are written in audience-neutral mechanics; per-group spins live in
   `drafts/` only.** A theme says "your members handle it, by your rules"; the
   chess-club / gardeners / neighbours / activists versions are drafting work, not
   theme content. (Keeps theme-05's on-ramp the single source of audience truth.)

Also locked: **never use the phrase "cancel culture" in lead copy** (research C2 —
partisan, toxic); describe the lived experience. FAQ-only if a reader raises it.
